Lumarie Calendar privacy policy

Effective November 6, 2026. This policy describes the personal household calendar application and these public information pages. Contact the operator at jesse.lumarie@gmail.com about access, deletion or other privacy questions.

Google access and purpose

The application requests calendar.calendarlist.readonly to list calendars accessible to the connected Google account and calendar.events.readonly to read events. These permissions can cover owned, shared and subscribed calendars. The local controls determine which calendars are fetched and displayed. Access is used to keep the household display current, format labels and handle duplicate display entries. No Google events or sharing permissions are changed.

The app stores calendar identifiers, labels, colors and access roles. For selected calendars, it retrieves event data and keeps identifiers, recurrence identifiers, titles, start/end times, all-day status, locations and private/cancellation flags. Attendee-response fields may be examined to recognize a declined event; descriptions and attendee lists are not retained in the normalized event cache or included in the AI requests described below.

Local storage and display

Calendar information, account identity, settings, Google authorization tokens, optional private-feed configuration, AI results and review choices are stored in files on the local computer. Calendar images are saved locally and sent over the household network to the wall display, where an image can remain stored and visible. People with access to the local controls, computer, backups or display may see calendar information.

The app uses private file permissions, an access code for local controls and a separate delivery token for display images. It does not encrypt its stored files, and local image delivery may use HTTP. It is intended to run on a private network. Diagnostic logs record operational metadata such as update times, status, image identifiers and transfer sizes.

Optional AI processing by OpenAI

When title shortening is enabled, source event titles, hashed request identifiers and character limits are sent through the Codex command-line tool to OpenAI's model service. When smart duplicate review is enabled, comparisons send calendar labels, titles, start/end times, all-day status, locations, private/cancellation flags and source freshness or missing-entry status, with a hashed comparison identifier. These features produce display labels or duplicate recommendations; they do not write to Google Calendar.

Titles, locations and calendar labels can contain names or other sensitive information. Hashing request identifiers does not anonymize that content. Entries marked private are excluded from new AI requests only when the app's hide-private setting is enabled; that setting does not erase previously stored or transmitted information. Disabling the relevant AI feature stops new work for that feature but cannot recall a request already sent.

Original titles, generated labels, comparison details and previous review information are cached locally. Temporary AI working files are removed when a request finishes. The application does not intentionally log AI prompts or responses. OpenAI's processing and retention depend on the Codex account, service terms and data controls; this application does not enforce a provider-wide retention or training setting. See OpenAI's privacy policy and the terms applicable to the connected service.

Retention, disconnection and deletion

Successful live refreshes replace that source's cached event range, normally the current month with a short surrounding window. Saved snapshots, failed or disconnected sources, AI caches, review history, backups and display images can retain information longer. There is no general automatic age-based deletion. Diagnostic logs rotate by size.

Disconnecting Google clears the app's local Google tokens and stops that OAuth connection. It preserves saved calendars, AI caches and images, and does not revoke the grant at Google. Remove the app through your Google Account's connected-app controls to revoke Google access. Separately configured private calendar feeds can continue until removed. Full local deletion requires the operator to remove the relevant saved data, backups and device images; contact the operator for help. Local deletion does not itself erase information already processed by an external provider.

Other disclosures and these pages

The app's functions do not include advertising or selling calendar data. Calendar information is not published on this site or sent to GitHub as part of calendar synchronization. These static information pages use no application analytics, forms or remote scripts. GitHub Pages hosts them and may process standard visitor request information under GitHub's privacy statement. Emailing support shares the information you choose to include with the operator and the email service.
